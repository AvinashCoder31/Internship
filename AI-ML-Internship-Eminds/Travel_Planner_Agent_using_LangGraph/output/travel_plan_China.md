## 5️⃣ Important Tips  

### A. Weather & Packing (August, 12‑13 °C, clear, light wind)  
* **Clothing:** Light jacket / windbreaker, comfortable walking shoes, scarf, small umbrella.  
* **Layers:** T‑shirts + long‑sleeve + sweater.  
* **Footwear:** Sneakers (break in), sandals/flip‑flops for beach, optional flip‑flops for hostel showers.  

### B. Transportation  
| Mode | When to Use | Money‑Saving Hacks |
|------|------------|-------------------|
| **Beijing Yikatong Card** | Metro, buses, some taxis | 10 % discount vs single‑ticket |
| **High‑Speed Rail (HSR)** | City‑to‑city (Beijing↔Xi’an, Xi’an↔Chengdu, Chengdu↔Guilin) | Book 2nd‑class via 12306; student/ youth discount if <26 |
| **Night Bus / Long‑Distance Bus** | Budget long‑distance (e.g., Chengdu→Sanya) | 60‑120 CNY; bring pillow, snacks |
| **Bike‑Sharing (Mobike/Ofo)** | Short hops (museum → market) | ~1 CNY per 15 min |
| **DiDi (Ride‑share)** | Late‑night, luggage | Use DiDi English UI; have 10 CNY cash backup |
| **Airport Transfer** | Airport → city | Airport bus (30‑40 CNY) or metro (8‑10 CNY) |

### C. Money & Payments  
* **WeChat Pay** & **Alipay** are the most universal.  Link a foreign card (Visa/MC) or purchase a **Chinese prepaid card** (e.g., UnionPay) for offline use.  
* Keep **200‑300 CNY cash** for street stalls that only accept cash.  
* **SIM card** (China Unicom 10 GB for 30 CNY) – buy at the airport for maps & payment apps.  

### D. Safety & Etiquette  
| Tip | Reason |
|-----|--------|
| Keep passport & a **digital copy** on phone & printed. | Required for train ticket pick‑up, ID checks. |
| Use **lock** for hostel lockers; bring a **small lock**. | Security in shared dorms. |
| Dress modestly at **temples & historic sites** (shoulders covered). | Respect local customs. |
| **No tipping** in most restaurants, but 5‑10 CNY for hostel staff or guides is appreciated. |
| Learn a few Mandarin phrases: “**请问…?**” (Excuse me...), “**多少钱?**” (How much?), “**谢谢**” (Thank you). | Locals appreciate the effort. |
| When using **public transport**, let passengers alight first, then board. | Polite local habit. |

### E. Cultural & Sociology “Mini‑Projects” (for your interest)  
* **Hutong Walk** – observe neighbor interactions, “door‑to‑door” life.  
* **Night Market** – compare bargaining styles in Beijing vs. Tianjin.  
* **Museum Visits** – note how history is presented (e.g., National Museum vs. Tianjin Museum).  
* **Take a notebook** and jot down **“social observations”** (e.g., “people on the Great Wall are mostly tourists vs. locals on the beach”).
